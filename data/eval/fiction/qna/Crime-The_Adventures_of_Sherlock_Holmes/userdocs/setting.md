# Setting - A Scandal in Bohemia

The story takes place in London in March 1888. Holmes's rooms at 221B Baker Street are the base of the investigation: a sitting room with a fire where clients are received, and a bedroom where Holmes keeps his disguises.

Briony Lodge is Irene Adler's small villa on Serpentine Avenue in St. John's Wood. It has two storeys, a garden at the back and a front built right up to the road. Beside one garden wall runs a lane with a mews, where grooms and cabmen look after horses and pass on local gossip.

Other places in the story are the Inner Temple, where Godfrey Norton has his chambers, and the Church of St. Monica in the Edgeware Road, where the wedding takes place.
