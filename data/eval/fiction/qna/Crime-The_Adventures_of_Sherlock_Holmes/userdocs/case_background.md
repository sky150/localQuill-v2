# Case Background - The King's Commission

Five years before the story, the then Crown Prince of Bohemia had an affair with Irene Adler in Warsaw. During that time they were photographed together. The King is now engaged to Clotilde Lothman von Saxe-Meningen, the second daughter of the King of Scandinavia, whose family is very strict. Adler has threatened to send them the photograph on the day the engagement is announced, which would end the marriage.

The King's agents have tried to get the photograph back five times: twice burglars searched her house, once her luggage was diverted while she travelled, and twice she was waylaid. None of these attempts found it. Buying it back has also failed, because Adler refuses to sell.

The King gives Holmes 300 pounds in gold and 700 pounds in notes for expenses and promises that he would give one of the provinces of his kingdom for the photograph. The photograph is cabinet-sized. Holmes reasons that Adler must keep it close at hand, because she plans to use it within days.
