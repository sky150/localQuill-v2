# Character Notes - A Scandal in Bohemia

## Sherlock Holmes
Consulting detective living at 221B Baker Street. Known for sharp observation, cold reasoning and a talent for disguise. He takes the King's case for the challenge as much as for the fee. In this story he is outwitted by a woman for the first time and afterwards speaks of Irene Adler with lasting respect.

## Dr. John Watson
Holmes's friend and former flatmate, and the narrator of the story. He has recently married and moved out of Baker Street, but visits Holmes again during this case. He admires Holmes's methods and helps him by throwing the smoke rocket at Briony Lodge.

## Irene Adler
Born in New Jersey, a former contralto at La Scala and prima donna of the Imperial Opera of Warsaw. She lives at Briony Lodge in St. John's Wood. Clever and quick, she sees through Holmes's disguise and leaves England before he can take the photograph.

## Godfrey Norton
A handsome, dark lawyer of the Inner Temple who visits Irene Adler often. He marries her in a hurried ceremony at the Church of St. Monica.

## The King of Bohemia
Wilhelm Gottsreich Sigismond von Ormstein, hereditary King of Bohemia. He first visits Holmes masked, calling himself Count Von Kramm. He is about to marry Clotilde Lothman von Saxe-Meningen and fears the scandal Adler's photograph could cause.

## The landlady
The landlady at Baker Street. In this story she is called Mrs. Turner, although in most other Holmes stories the landlady is Mrs. Hudson.
