# Mina's Condition

Dracula visited Mina at night over several days, and in his final attack he forced her to drink his blood from a cut on his chest. Since then she has been slowly changing. She grows pale, sleeps more and finds her teeth sharpening.

When Van Helsing pressed a piece of the sacred wafer to her forehead to protect her, it burned her and left a red scar. The scar is a mark of the Count's power over her.

The blood bond works in two directions. Dracula can learn what she knows, so the men must sometimes keep plans from her. But Mina can also sense where the Count is. Under Van Helsing's hypnosis at sunrise and sunset, she describes sounds and surroundings that reveal his location, such as lapping water and creaking timbers. This has shown the hunters that he is on a ship at sea.
