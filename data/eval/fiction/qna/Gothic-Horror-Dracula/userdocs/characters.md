# Character Notes - Dracula

## Mina Harker (née Murray)
A schoolmistress, Jonathan Harker's wife, and Lucy Westenra's closest friend. Intelligent and organised, she types up and arranges the group's diaries and letters into one record. After Dracula's attack she is slowly changing, but she keeps fighting and helps the hunters.

## Jonathan Harker
A young solicitor from Exeter who was sent to Transylvania to arrange the Count's purchase of Carfax estate. He escaped from Castle Dracula. His hair turned white from the shock of what happened. He is devoted to Mina.

## Dr. John Seward
Director of a lunatic asylum near Carfax and a former suitor of Lucy. He keeps his diary on a phonograph. He narrates many chapters.

## Professor Abraham Van Helsing
A Dutch doctor, philosopher and Seward's old teacher. He knows the most about vampires and leads the group. He gave Lucy peace after her death and now guides the hunt for Dracula.

## Arthur Holmwood, Lord Godalming
Lucy's fiancé, who inherited his father's title. He drove the stake through Lucy's heart. His money and position help the group.

## Quincey Morris
A brave, plain-speaking American from Texas and another former suitor of Lucy. He carries a large Bowie knife and is loyal to his friends.

## Count Dracula
An ancient Transylvanian noble and vampire who has come to England. He can command animals, change form and influence those whose blood he has taken.

## Lucy Westenra
Mina's friend, who was Dracula's first victim in England. She died, became a vampire and was destroyed by the men.
