# Setting - Late in Dracula

By the late chapters the hunters are based at Dr. Seward's asylum at Purfleet, east of London, next to Carfax, the estate Dracula bought. They have searched Dracula's houses in London and destroyed most of his boxes of earth. The Count has escaped and is sailing back towards the Black Sea on a ship carrying his last box.

The group meets in Seward's study, where they plan their next steps. They are preparing to travel across Europe to Varna and on into Transylvania to stop Dracula before he reaches his castle. The diary entries are dated to October, and the hunters are aware that time is running out.
