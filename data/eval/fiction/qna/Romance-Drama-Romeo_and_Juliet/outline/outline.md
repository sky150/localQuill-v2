# Romeo and Juliet - Outline

William Shakespeare's tragedy (around 1595) is set in Verona, where the Montague and Capulet families have a long and violent feud. Romeo, a young Montague, sneaks into a Capulet feast and falls in love with Juliet, the Capulets' daughter. They meet again that night at her balcony and agree to marry, and Friar Lawrence marries them in secret the next day, hoping the match will end the feud.

Soon afterwards Juliet's cousin Tybalt kills Romeo's friend Mercutio in a street fight, and Romeo kills Tybalt in revenge. The Prince banishes Romeo to Mantua. Juliet's parents, unaware of her marriage, arrange for her to marry Count Paris. Friar Lawrence gives Juliet a potion that will make her appear dead for a time, so she can escape the wedding, and he sends a letter to tell Romeo of the plan. The letter never reaches him.

Romeo's servant Balthasar tells him that Juliet is dead. Romeo buys poison from an apothecary and goes to the Capulet tomb, where he fights and kills Paris. He then drinks the poison beside Juliet and dies. Juliet wakes, finds Romeo dead, and stabs herself with his dagger. Friar Lawrence explains what happened, and the Montagues and Capulets, grieving for their children, end their feud.
