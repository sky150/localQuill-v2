# Setting - The Capulet Tomb

The scene takes place at night in a churchyard in Verona, where the Capulet family monument stands. The monument is a stone vault where the family's dead are laid. Juliet was placed there after her apparent death, and her cousin Tybalt, recently killed, also lies inside.

The churchyard is dark and quiet. The ground is loose from the digging of graves, and yew trees grow there. The visitors carry torches, which can be seen from far away.

At this point in the play, Romeo has travelled from Mantua after hearing of Juliet's death. He does not know that she is only asleep under the effect of the Friar's potion and will soon wake up.
