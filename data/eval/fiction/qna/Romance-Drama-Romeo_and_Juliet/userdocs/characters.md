# Character Notes - Romeo and Juliet

## Romeo
The only son of Lord and Lady Montague. Passionate and impulsive, he married Juliet in secret. He was banished from Verona to Mantua for killing Tybalt.

## Juliet
The thirteen-year-old daughter of Lord and Lady Capulet. She married Romeo in secret. To escape a forced marriage to Paris, she took Friar Lawrence's potion, which makes her appear dead. She has been laid in the Capulet family tomb.

## Count Paris
A young nobleman and kinsman of the Prince and of Mercutio. He was engaged to marry Juliet with her father's approval and sincerely mourns her.

## Balthasar
Romeo's loyal servant. He brought Romeo the news of Juliet's death in Mantua.

## Friar Lawrence
A Franciscan friar and Romeo's adviser. He married Romeo and Juliet and gave Juliet the sleeping potion. His letter explaining the plan to Romeo was never delivered.

## Tybalt
Juliet's hot-tempered cousin. He killed Mercutio and was then killed by Romeo. He lies in the Capulet tomb.

## Mercutio
Romeo's witty friend and a kinsman of the Prince. He was killed by Tybalt.

## Paris's Page
A boy who serves Paris and keeps watch for him.
