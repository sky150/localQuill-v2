# The Wonderful Wizard of Oz - Outline

L. Frank Baum's novel (1900) follows Dorothy, a girl from Kansas, who is carried with her dog Toto to the Land of Oz by a cyclone. Her house lands on the Wicked Witch of the East and kills her. Dorothy receives the Witch's silver shoes, and the Good Witch of the North tells her to follow the road of yellow brick to the Emerald City, where the great Wizard Oz may be able to send her home.

On the way she is joined by the Scarecrow, who wants brains, the Tin Woodman, who wants a heart, and the Cowardly Lion, who wants courage. The group faces dangers on the road, such as ditches, the Kalidahs, a river and a field of deadly poppies. In the Emerald City, the Wizard will only help them if they kill the Wicked Witch of the West. The Witch captures Dorothy and the Lion, but Dorothy melts her by throwing a bucket of water over her, and the Winkies are freed.

Back in the Emerald City, the Wizard turns out to be an ordinary man from Omaha. He gives the Scarecrow, Tin Woodman and Lion symbolic gifts that show they already had what they wanted. His balloon leaves without Dorothy, so the friends travel south to Glinda, the Good Witch of the South. Glinda tells Dorothy that the silver shoes can carry her home. The Scarecrow, Tin Woodman and Lion each become rulers in Oz, and Dorothy clicks her heels together three times and returns to Kansas.
