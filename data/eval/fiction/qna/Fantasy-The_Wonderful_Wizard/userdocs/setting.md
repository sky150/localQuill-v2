# Setting - The Land of Oz

Oz is a magical country cut off from the rest of the world by a great desert. It is divided into four regions: the Munchkin Country in the east (blue), the Winkie Country in the west (yellow), the Gillikin Country in the north (purple) and the Quadling Country in the south (red). At its centre lies the Emerald City, where everything looks green.

The road of yellow brick leads from the Munchkin Country to the Emerald City. Part of the way it runs through a thick, dark forest that is cut by deep ditches with steep sides and sharp rocks at the bottom. Dangerous beasts such as the Kalidahs live in this forest. Beyond the forest a broad river separates the woods from a pleasant country of green meadows, flowers and fruit trees.

Animals in Oz can talk, and creatures like the Scarecrow and the Tin Woodman are alive without needing food or sleep.
