# Character Notes - The Wonderful Wizard of Oz

## Dorothy
A young girl from a farm in Kansas who lives with her Aunt Em and Uncle Henry. Kind, practical and determined to get home. She wears the silver shoes of the Wicked Witch of the East and carries a basket for food on the journey.

## Toto
Dorothy's small black dog, who keeps her cheerful. He travels everywhere in her arms or at her feet.

## The Scarecrow
Made of straw and found on a pole in a cornfield. He believes he has no brains and wants the Wizard to give him some, yet he often comes up with the group's best plans. He cannot be hurt by falling and does not get tired or hungry, but he fears fire.

## The Tin Woodman
A woodman made entirely of tin, who wants a heart. He is very tender-hearted and careful not to hurt any living creature. If he cries, his jaws rust, so he needs his oil-can. His sharp axe is often useful on the journey.

## The Cowardly Lion
A large lion who believes he is a coward and wants courage. He roars to frighten others but is often afraid himself. In practice he acts bravely when his friends are in danger, and his size and strength help the group many times.

## The Wizard of Oz
The ruler of the Emerald City, said to be great and terrible. The travellers hope he will grant their wishes.
