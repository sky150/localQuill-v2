# Setting - Elsinore

The play takes place at Elsinore, the royal castle of Denmark on the coast, guarded by soldiers on its battlements at night. The court is full of watchers: courtiers, spies and messengers report to the King, and conversations are often overheard.

Shortly before the scene, Rosencrantz and Guildenstern have reported to the King that they could not find the cause of Hamlet's strange behaviour, but that he was pleased by the arrival of a company of travelling actors. Hamlet has asked the actors to perform a play for the court that evening.

The scene takes place in a room of the castle, where the King and Polonius have arranged for Hamlet to meet Ophelia as if by chance.
