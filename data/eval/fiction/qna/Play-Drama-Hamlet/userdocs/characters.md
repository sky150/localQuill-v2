# Character Notes - Hamlet

## Hamlet
Prince of Denmark and son of the late King Hamlet. He returned from university in Wittenberg for his father's funeral. Thoughtful, witty and bitter, he has been acting strangely since his father's ghost told him he was murdered. Whether his madness is real or acted is one of the play's central questions.

## Claudius
The King of Denmark, Hamlet's uncle. He killed his brother, took the throne and married the widowed queen. He is a skilful politician but troubled by guilt. He watches Hamlet with growing suspicion.

## Gertrude
The Queen of Denmark and Hamlet's mother. She married Claudius soon after her first husband's death. She loves her son and hopes his madness can be cured.

## Polonius
The Lord Chamberlain and chief counsellor to the King. He talks a lot and likes to spy. He believes Hamlet's madness is caused by love for Ophelia.

## Ophelia
Polonius's daughter, whom Hamlet has courted. Obedient to her father, she has stopped seeing Hamlet at his order. She is caught between her love for Hamlet and her duty to her family.

## Rosencrantz and Guildenstern
Childhood friends of Hamlet, summoned by the King and Queen to find out what troubles him. Hamlet soon suspects they are working for the King.

## Horatio
Hamlet's loyal friend from university and the only person he fully trusts.

## Laertes
Polonius's son and Ophelia's brother. He is in France for most of the first half of the play.
