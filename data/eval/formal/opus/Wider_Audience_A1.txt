I drive a car for work every day. I find the traffic problem so boring. The amount of traffic 
increased a lot last year, because last year I spent 45 minutes driving to work, but nowadays I spend 
1 hour and 30 minutes. People like to buy new cars, and they love to show their high social status. Some people 
like to keep their old cars. Both kinds of people make the traffic problems worse. If the government wants to reduce 
the amount of traffic, it should offer new ways to travel, for example by adding more buses. However, we still have 
a problem: why do people like to travel by car?
