 The first text suggests that nowadays the population is experiencing the new 
innovations and technologies at an extremely fast tempo. This process has a massive

around them. Our response and positive attitude are crucial if we are willing to

training is highly important as it gives a chance to get familiar with the upcoming 
innovations before using them on a daily basis. As a result, people will experience

 Both texts show the importance of accepting the new technologies. It 
does come with a variety of different benefits for the entire population.

exploring and allowing the new innovations to become part of our lives without
