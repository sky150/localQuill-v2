Hello. Recently, I’ve been concerned about the increase in the number of cars in our neighbourhood. 
This explosive increase is causing air pollution, noise, and traffic accidents. I think we have to put a 
brake on this increase. To stop this increase, we have to know the reason why this increase is 
happening.  There are three reasons. The first is the inconvenience of the metro. We have to propose some 
improvements to the metro system to the metro company, such as ticket-buying systems, the 
number of trains, etc. If these problems are solved, I’m pretty sure that the amount of traffic will 
decrease. The second reason is the width of the roads. Most of the roads in this town are too narrow 
for cars to pass. So these narrow roads must be widened in order to reduce the amount of traffic. 
And the last reason is the fall in the price of gasoline. These days, the price of gasoline is falling so 
rapidly. I think the price of gasoline should be fixed in order to reduce this lorry traffic.  I hope the metro companies and the municipality will read my comment.
