Hello everyone!  It is nothing new that traffic is increasing in our town; day by day, the number of cars and lorries grows 
in the neighborhood. Car traffic is not a big problem for me. Sometimes it is bad, but we can live with 
this situation. The big problem is the lorry traffic.  Lorries are very noisy: when they pass along the street, we can hear them inside our houses, and this is very 
annoying because they also pass at night. Another issue is that the streets in this town are not 
wide, so when a truck is passing, the other drivers cannot drive in the same street, which causes confusion 
and more noise. It is difficult for the lorry drivers too, because it is really hard and dangerous to drive a 
huge truck in a small street.  A possible solution to this problem would be to redirect the heavy traffic to wider 
streets, like avenues. In the small streets, we should keep only the local traffic and buses.  Please write your comments and opinions about the situation and help us have a good town. Thank you.
