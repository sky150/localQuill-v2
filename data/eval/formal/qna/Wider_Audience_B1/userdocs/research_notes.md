# Research Notes - Lorry Traffic in Towns

Heavy goods vehicles (lorries or trucks) are much louder than cars, especially when they accelerate, brake or drive at night. Night-time traffic noise can disturb sleep and has been linked to stress and health problems.

Narrow streets in old town centres were not built for large vehicles. Lorries in these streets can block other traffic, damage buildings and pavements, and create dangerous situations for pedestrians and cyclists.

Many towns use measures such as lorry routes on main roads, weight or size limits on residential streets, time restrictions for deliveries, and ring roads or bypasses that keep through traffic away from the centre.
