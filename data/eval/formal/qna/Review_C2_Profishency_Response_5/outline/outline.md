# Outline - A Book from My Childhood (Review)

Task: A magazine for young people is asking readers to send in reviews of a book that brings back memories of their childhood. Describe the book, explain why it is special to you, and say whether you think it is still relevant for young readers today.

Planned structure: introduce the book, its author and its place in a larger story. Then explain why the book is special, with a short outline of the plot and its most exciting parts. Describe the main character and why he is admirable. Finish with the values the book teaches and a recommendation for today's young readers.

The review should be personal and enthusiastic while giving the reader enough information to decide whether to read the book.
