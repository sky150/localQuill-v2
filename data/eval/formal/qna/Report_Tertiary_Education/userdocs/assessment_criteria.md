# Assessment Criteria - Tertiary Project Report

## Problem and research question
The report states a clear problem, explains why it matters and formulates a research question that can be answered with the chosen method.

## Background and sources
The state of the art is explained accurately and supported by cited sources. Direct quotations are marked and referenced.

## Method and results
The methods are described in enough detail to be repeated. Results are presented clearly and linked back to the research question.

## Structure and language
The report follows a logical structure with numbered sections and gives an overview of the structure in the introduction. The language is formal, precise and objective, and technical terms are explained when first used.
