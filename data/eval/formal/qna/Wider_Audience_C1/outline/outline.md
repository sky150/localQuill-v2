# Outline - Comment on Traffic in Your Town

Task: A local website has asked residents to comment on the recent increase in traffic in their town or city. Write a comment for the website. Describe the traffic problem as you see it, explain how it affects you or other people, and suggest what could be done to reduce the amount of traffic.

Planned structure: start by introducing the problem and why it matters to you. Then give one or more reasons or effects with a personal example. End with at least one practical suggestion and a short closing sentence that invites a response or expresses a hope for change.

The comment should be clear to other residents and the local authorities. At C1 level, the comment should be well structured, use a formal complaint register and support each point with specific details.
