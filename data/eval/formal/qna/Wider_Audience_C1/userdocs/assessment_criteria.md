# Assessment Criteria - C1 Writing

Cambridge English marks writing on four subscales: Content, Communicative Achievement, Organisation and Language.

## Content
The writer fully develops the problem with specific causes and effects and offers clear, relevant demands, so the reader is fully informed.

## Communicative Achievement
The text should suit its purpose and audience. For a comment on a local website, the writer addresses other residents or the authorities directly and keeps a suitable, mostly neutral tone.

## Organisation
Ideas should be connected and follow a clear order: the problem, its causes or effects, and a suggested solution.

## Language
The writer uses a wide range of vocabulary, including less common expressions, and a variety of complex structures with good control. Errors are rare and do not affect communication.
