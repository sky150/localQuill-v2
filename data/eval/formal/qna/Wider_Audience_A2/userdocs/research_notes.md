# Research Notes - Traffic in Bangkok

Bangkok is the capital and largest city of Thailand, with around 10 million people in the city and more in the wider metropolitan area. It is known for some of the worst traffic congestion in Asia.

Public transport in Bangkok includes the BTS Skytrain, the MRT underground and elevated lines, buses, river boats and motorcycle taxis. The rail network has grown a lot since the first Skytrain line opened in 1999, but many people still drive.

Car exhaust contains carbon dioxide (CO2), which is a greenhouse gas that contributes to global warming. The hole in the ozone layer was mainly caused by chemicals such as CFCs, not by CO2.

Carpooling, where people who travel the same route share one car, can reduce the number of vehicles on the road and the cost of travel for each person.
