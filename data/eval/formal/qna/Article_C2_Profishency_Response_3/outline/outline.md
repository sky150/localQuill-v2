# Outline - An Unforgettable Event (Article)

Task: An international magazine has invited readers to send in an article called "An Unforgettable Event". Describe an event in your life that you will never forget, explain why it was so memorable, and say what effect remembering important events can have on people.

Planned structure: open with a vivid account of the moment the event began, using direct speech to bring it to life. Then describe what happened next and what made it special. Add a personal reflection on which part of the experience stays with you most. Finish with a general paragraph on why recalling memorable events is good for us.

The article should engage a wide magazine readership, so the tone can be personal and lively while staying well organised.
