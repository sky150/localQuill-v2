# Research Notes - Generations and Technology

Surveys regularly find that younger adults adopt new devices and apps faster than older adults, but the gap has narrowed as smartphones and internet use have spread to all age groups. Older users often learn new technology successfully when they get patient, practical support.

Researchers warn against treating age as the only factor. Previous experience, education, confidence and access to training also shape how quickly people adapt.

In workplaces, training that lets employees practise with new tools before they use them in real tasks reduces errors and stress and makes staff more willing to accept the change.
