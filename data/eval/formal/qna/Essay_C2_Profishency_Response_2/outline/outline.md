# Outline - New Technologies (Essay)

Task: Read two texts about how people respond to new technology. Write an essay summarising and evaluating the key points from both texts, using your own words throughout as far as possible, and include your own ideas in your answer. Write 240-280 words.

Planned structure: summarise the first text on the speed of technological change and the different reactions of younger and older people. Then summarise the second text on the need for time and training, especially at work. Compare what both texts have in common, and finish with a conclusion that gives your own view on how society should approach new innovations.

The essay should balance both texts, connect them clearly and use a formal register.
