# Source Texts - Notes

## Text 1: Keeping up with technology
New technologies are being introduced faster than ever before. Younger people tend to adapt quickly, while older people often need more time before they feel comfortable. The writer argues that a positive and open attitude towards innovation is essential if society wants to make progress and build a better future for the next generation.

## Text 2: Technology at work
People should not feel pushed to use new technology before they are ready. Research suggests that how well someone copes with technological change depends partly on personality: people who feel in control of their lives tend to welcome change, while less confident people may feel threatened. In the workplace, proper training gives employees time to practise and become familiar with new tools, which leads to more effective ways of working.
