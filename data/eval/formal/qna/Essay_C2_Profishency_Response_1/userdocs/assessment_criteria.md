# Assessment Criteria - C2 Proficiency Writing

Cambridge English marks writing on four subscales, each scored from 0 to 5.

## Content
All content is relevant to the task, and the target reader is fully informed.

## Communicative Achievement
The writer uses the conventions of the task type with flexibility and holds the reader's attention. Straightforward and complex ideas are communicated effectively, and the register suits the purpose.

## Organisation
The text is well organised and coherent. A wide range of cohesive devices and organisational patterns is used with complete flexibility.

## Language
A wide range of vocabulary, including less common lexis, is used with fluency, precision and sophistication. Simple and complex grammatical forms are used with full control. Errors are rare and difficult to spot.
