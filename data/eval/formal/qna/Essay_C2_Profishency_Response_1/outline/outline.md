# Outline - Man vs. Computer (Essay)

Task: Read two texts about how people respond to new technology. Write an essay summarising and evaluating the key points from both texts, using your own words throughout as far as possible, and include your own ideas in your answer. Write 240-280 words.

Planned structure: introduce technology as part of everyday life and its benefits. Then discuss the people who are uncomfortable with fast technological change and what research says about why. Move on to the consequences for the workplace and argue for effective, practical training. Conclude with the first step employers should take and why it matters for the future.

The essay should evaluate the ideas rather than simply repeat them, and it should use a formal register suitable for an academic reader.
