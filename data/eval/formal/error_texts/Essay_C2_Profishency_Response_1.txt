MAN VS. COMPUTER:
It is a undeniable truth that technology have become a mainstay in our lifes. From our 
personal lives to our public sphere tasks there is a perpetual flow of gadget and 
gizmos creeping in to our environments to assist us with our activitys. And whats not 
to like? For the most part even the most monotonous of tasks have became more 
quicker and easier with the help of this inventions.
Of course, there is those who are not yet complete 
comfortable with this notion. The sheer acceleration with which new technologies is 
brought to life are enough to make any tradition-oriented person runs for 
the hills! In fact, there are studies which it has been proven that an individuals ability 
to ‘cope’ with this phenomenon to mirrors there character; people 
who holds tighter reins on the happenings in their 
lives tend to welcome the change whereas those who are less confident see this it as 
a threat to a certain degree.
This leads too a impasse in the workplace; an employee probably 
cannot avoid working with new technology all together first simply because they is 
less confident. Therefore it is imperative that works are effective and practically 
trained so that they can been in control, and thus have no qualms about 
new innovations. The first step here would be to instill the right mindset in employee's 
by assuring them that they will would gain great from this change. In fact, this is a 
vital step in any effort to seamlessly integrate humans and technology, as it 
pave the way for far greater developments in the future.
