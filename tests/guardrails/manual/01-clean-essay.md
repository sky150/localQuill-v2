# The Industrial Revolution

The industrial revolution fundamentally transformed British society. New machinery moved
work from cottages into factories, and families followed the work into growing towns.
Wages rose slowly, but living conditions in the new cities were often poor.

Historians still debate whether ordinary workers benefitted in the short term. What is
clear is that the pattern of daily life, from the length of the working day to the food on
the table, changed for good within two generations.
