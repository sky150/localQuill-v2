# Essay outline: Technology in the workplace

Task: C2 Proficiency essay, 240–280 words. Summarise and evaluate the two texts on how people adapt to new technology.

## 1. Introduction
- Hook: technology has become part of everyday working life.
- Background: new tools arrive faster than ever, and not everyone feels comfortable with them.
- Thesis: people adapt to technology at different speeds, so employers should support them with training rather than force change.

## 2. Body paragraph 1: Text one
- Summary: younger people adapt quickly, older people need more time.
- Evaluation: the text is optimistic but generalises by age.
- Example: an older colleague who learned a new booking system within a week.

## 3. Body paragraph 2: Text two
- Summary: society should not feel rushed; proper training in the workplace is essential.
- Evaluation: the strongest point of both texts, because it gives a practical solution.
- Link back to the thesis: training builds confidence.

## 4. Counterargument
- Some argue that training costs too much time and money for small companies.
- Response: untrained staff make costly mistakes, so training pays for itself.

## 5. Conclusion
- Restate the thesis in new words.
- Final thought: technology should serve people, not the other way round.
- Keep the conclusion to three sentences and do not add new arguments.

## Style notes
- Formal register: no contractions, no first person in the body paragraphs.
- Use linking words between paragraphs (however, in contrast, consequently).
